Answers About DEWA Approvals in Dubai

Clear answers directly from our DEWA approval engineers.

To submit a DEWA Fit-Out NOC application, you will need: (1) Copy of the Tenancy Contract (Ejari) or Title Deed, (2) Landlord / Building Management NOC letter, (3) Architectural interior layout drawing in DWG/PDF, (4) Certified Single-Line Diagram (SLD) prepared by qualified engineers, (5) Total connected electrical load schedule, and (6) The unit Premise ID (found on the green plate at your doorstep or existing bill). Our team handles all drawing preparation and portal submission on your behalf.

A standard DEWA Fit-Out NOC review typically takes 3 to 5 business days following submission on the DEWA E-Services portal. If your project involves a power load enhancement or substation modification, the timeline typically ranges between 7 to 14 business days depending on existing building transformer margin.

A DEWA inspection officer physically visits your property to verify that: (1) Cabling matches the approved Single-Line Diagram, (2) Cable sizes, breaker ratings, and RCD sensitivities match DEWA specifications, (3) Earth continuity and earthing pit resistance measure less than 1.0 Ohm, (4) Proper phase segregation and ferrule labels are installed, and (5) The meter cabinet conforms to spatial clearance rules. We perform calibrated pre-testing before the inspector arrives and accompany them on site to guarantee a first-time pass.
