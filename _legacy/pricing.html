Understanding Official DEWA Government Fees

In addition to our engineering and liaison fees, DEWA assesses statutory utility fees depending on your scope.

1. Fit-Out Review / NOC Examination Fee

DEWA typically charges a statutory NOC application processing fee (usually AED 200 - AED 500 depending on category) directly payable through your DEWA account portal upon drawing submission.

2. Additional Connected Load Charges (kW Capacity Fee)

If you are requesting a power load upgrade (extra kW) that exceeds the building’s original allocation, DEWA charges an official capacity tariff per additional kW sanctioned. We optimize your diversity calculations so you never pay for unnecessary kilowatt capacity.

3. Smart Meter Fee & Security Deposit

Upon final inspection pass, a new smart meter installation fee and tenant refundable security deposit are billed to the tenant's Ejari account (typically AED 2,000 for commercial apartments/shops, AED 4,000 for villas and larger units).

4. Inspection Re-Visit Fee (If Failed by Other Contractors)

When uncertified fit-out companies fail their first inspection, DEWA levies a re-inspection penalty fee. With Abbadi Tech, our 100% pre-testing guarantee prevents this penalty completely.
