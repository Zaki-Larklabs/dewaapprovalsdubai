# Content briefs

Status: Gate A. These briefs define what each page must contain. They do not authorise publication. Every procedural page needs an official source, a named reviewer and a review date.

## 1. Editorial standard

- Answer first. State the dependency before the sales message.
- Use short paragraphs, concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Name the authority, the document and the next action.
- Use “usually”, “can” or “depends on” only when the next sentence names the dependency.
- Do not write “fast”, “trusted”, “guaranteed”, “hassle-free”, “seamless” or “end-to-end” unless the claim is verified.
- Do not imply that Abbadi Tech is DEWA.
- Do not publish a fee or timeline without a scope condition and a source.
- Every answer is 40–90 words unless the format requires more.
- Every procedural page includes the independence notice.
- Arabic is a human-reviewed translation, not raw machine output.

## 2. Page template briefs

### Home

**Job:** explain the consultancy, the route and the next step in one screen.

**Must include:**

- H1 with the category, not a slogan.
- One-sentence positioning that names independence.
- Primary CTA: start a project file.
- Secondary CTA: find the approval route.
- Technical drawing panel, clearly illustrative.
- Trust strip: independent, Dubai, bilingual, documented.
- Approval pathfinder with property, activity and change.
- Service register with 12 scopes.
- Project-type matrix ranked by dependency.
- Six-stage process with owners and outputs.
- Proof slot labelled as a layout proof until a verified case exists.
- Coverage index with internal links.
- FAQ preview with 8 questions.
- Quote form.
- Independence notice and title block in the footer.

**Do not:** claim “trusted”, “fast”, “No. 1”, a project count, a success rate or a guarantee.

**Length:** 1,200–1,800 words of visible copy, excluding form labels and tables.

### Service page

**Job:** answer one approval scope and prove the file can be prepared correctly.

**Must include:**

- H1 with the service name.
- 40–90 word answer box.
- Who commonly needs it.
- What the authority reviews.
- Document checklist.
- Sequence and dependencies.
- Adjacent approvals, named as adjacent.
- Common reviewer comments or failure modes.
- Inspection or hand-off notes.
- 4–6 FAQs.
- Sources with publisher and access date.
- Reviewer name, role and review date.
- Related services and project types.
- CTA to start a project file.

**Do not:** reuse the same 12 paragraphs across services. Do not call every step a DEWA step. Do not promise an approval outcome.

**Length:** 1,100–1,700 words.

### Project-type page

**Job:** show the order of approvals for a real project type.

**Must include:**

- H1 with the project type.
- 40–90 word answer box.
- Ranked approval sequence with dependency and output.
- Document checklist.
- Load or demand drivers.
- Common pitfalls and controls.
- Inspection hand-off.
- Verified project slot or a labelled placeholder.
- 4–6 FAQs.
- Sources and review block.
- Sidebar summary.
- CTA.

**Length:** 900–1,500 words.

### Area page

**Job:** explain what changes locally, without becoming a doorway page.

**Must include:**

- H1 with the area and approval intent.
- Local property and activity mix.
- Authority boundary: DEWA, landlord/master developer, free zone, Municipality, Civil Defence.
- Common constraints: access, existing supply, asset corridors, older buildings, master-community rules.
- Related services.
- One verified local project or a clear statement that none is published.
- 3–5 local FAQs.
- Sources and review block.
- CTA.

**Do not publish** an area page that only swaps the place name into a template. If there is no local content, keep it in draft or do not create it.

**Length:** 700–1,200 words.

### FAQ page and category page

**Job:** answer a specific question and link to the deeper page.

**Must include:**

- One question per `<details>` item.
- 40–90 word answer.
- The dependency, the document and the next action.
- Source links.
- Reviewer and review date.
- Links to the relevant service, project type or area.
- A correction route.

**Do not:** duplicate the same answer across categories. Do not publish a FAQPage schema for answers that are not visible, unique and reviewed.

### About / how we work

**Job:** establish the entity, method and independence.

**Must include:**

- Legal entity and trading name, once verified.
- Independence notice.
- Who does what: editor, reviewer, consultant, contractor.
- Document control method.
- Review and correction policy.
- Contact route.
- No invented team photos, credentials, client logos or project counts.

### Contact

**Job:** collect a project file and set a clear next step.

**Must include:**

- Form fields: name, company, email, phone/WhatsApp, service, project type, area, premise/customer number, what is changing, upload, consent.
- Privacy link.
- Direct contact details, once verified.
- Response expectation, once verified.
- Independence notice.
- No promise of a quote or approval.

### Legal

Plain-language privacy, terms, cookies, accessibility and editorial/corrections pages. Legal review required before publication.

## 5. Area briefs

Phase 1 areas: Business Bay, Al Quoz, Dubai Investment Park, Dubai South, JLT, Deira, Al Barsha, Dubai Silicon Oasis.

Each brief must answer:

- What property and activity mix is typical here?
- Which authority or master developer controls the building or community?
- What usually complicates a fit-out or connection in this area?
- Which services are most relevant?
- What local evidence or project can be published, if any?
- Which questions should be answered locally rather than repeated from the service page?

No area page publishes with only a map, a list of services and a swapped place name.

## 7. Content quality gate

A page is ready for review only when:

- The answer box is unique and 40–90 words.
- The document list is specific to the service or project type.
- The sequence names dependencies and adjacent approvals.
- The failure modes are concrete.
- Every procedural statement has an official source.
- The reviewer and review date are populated.
- Every claim is verified in `docs/claims-to-verify.md`.
- Internal links point to the right service, project type, area or FAQ category.
- The title, H1 and meta description are unique.
- The page has no `[VERIFY]` placeholder.
- The Arabic version, if in scope, is human-reviewed.
- The independence notice is present.
- No reference copy, image or entity name is used.
