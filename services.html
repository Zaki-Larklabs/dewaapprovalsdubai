3. Certified Single-Line Diagrams (SLD) Design

The Single-Line Diagram is the electrical blueprint required by DEWA for any installation. It dictates cable gauges, breaker sensitivities, phase distribution (Red, Yellow, Blue), and discrimination. Our in-house licensed engineers engineer compliant SLDs matching Dubai regulations.

Technical Standards Followed:

  • BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) & DEWA Green Building Regulations
  • Earth leakage protection (30mA for socket outlets, 100mA/300mA for machinery)
  • Phase balancing calculations preventing neutral overheating
  • Short circuit fault current withstand capacity (kA rating)
Order Stamped SLD Drawing →

What DEWA Checks in SLDs:

  • ✓ Incomer isolator rating & cable size matching source
  • ✓ 3-Phase balanced kW distribution
  • ✓ RCD trip sensitivity for wet and dry circuits
  • ✓ Earthing schematic with earth pit details (<1.0Ω)
  • ✓ Official engineering stamp of certified contractor
